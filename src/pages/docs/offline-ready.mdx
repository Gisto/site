---
title: 'Is Gisto fully offline-ready?'
author: Sasha Khamkov
category: 2. F.A.Q.
published: 2026-05-29
---

Yes. You can use local storage to save snippets directly in your browser without an account. Gisto uses IndexedDB to store snippets locally. You can create, edit, tag, and query all your snippets without any active internet connection.

This is a local-only mode - snippets are stored in your browser and **will not sync** across devices or to any cloud provider. To move snippets between devices or back them up, use **export and import as JSON** from Settings.
