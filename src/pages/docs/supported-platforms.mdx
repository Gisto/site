---
title: 'Does Gisto support other platforms besides GitHub?'
author: Sasha Khamkov
category: 2. F.A.Q.
published: 2026-05-29
---

Yes, Gisto supports multiple providers. You can choose to connect GitHub Gists, GitLab Snippets, [Snippet-Bin](https://github.com/sanusart/snippet-bin) (a self-hosted snippet server), or use local storage to save snippets directly in your browser — one provider at a time, with the flexibility to switch anytime.
