## Overview

Beyond GitHub, Gisto now supports GitLab Snippets, expanding your code snippet sources and workflows. Connect your GitLab account and use all your existing snippets by connecting your GitLab account. You can access your snippets from GitLab Snippets at any time with changes carrying over to Gisto.

## Connecting Your GitLab Account

To connect your GitLab account:

1. Open Gisto login page
2. Select "GitLab" as your provider
3. Insert GitLab Personal Access Token

## Working with GitLab Snippets

Once connected, your GitLab snippets will appear. You can:

- **Search and filter** snippets using the same search interface
- **Create new snippets** on GitLab directly through Gisto
- **Edit existing snippets** stored on GitLab
- **Tag and organize** your GitLab snippets the same way as other snippets
