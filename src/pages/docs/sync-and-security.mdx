---
title: "How does Gisto sync with GitHub Gists & GitLab? Is my data secure?"
author: Sasha Khamkov
category: 2. F.A.Q.
published: 2026-05-29
---

Gisto connects directly to the official GitHub & GitLab APIs via Personal Access Tokens. Gisto operates on a local-first offline architecture — your tokens, database files, and markdown configurations are stored locally on your own machine. Your data never touches any Gisto-owned external servers.
